Demystifying Generative AI & Large Language Models (LLMs)

Generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot do not possess consciousness, genuine understanding, or emotions. Instead, they are high-dimensional statistical pattern-matching engines trained on vast datasets of human text and code.

Traditional Software

Rule-based. Runs strict logic: If X happens, do Y exactly as programmed. Deterministic outputs.

Generative AI (LLMs)

Probability-based. Predicts the most likely next word (token) based on context and trained patterns. Probabilistic outputs.
